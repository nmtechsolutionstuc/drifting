## Stack

## Users

Buyers of unisex streetwear clothing — jeans baggy, camperas, buzos, remeras, gorras — based in and around Tucumán (Juan Bautista Alberdi) and nationally across Argentina (envíos a todo el país). Style-conscious, drawn to a "tu estilo, tu flow" attitude-and-self-expression positioning rather than generic basics.

## Product Purpose

A conversion-focused landing page for the brand Drifting. It has no on-site checkout — the funnel routes visitors to WhatsApp (3865 66-64-33) and Instagram (@drifting.ind) to inquire and buy. Success is visitors browsing the collection and reaching out via WhatsApp/Instagram, or finding the physical location (Juan Bautista Alberdi, Tucumán) on the map.

## Positioning

Unisex streetwear with attitude and "flow" — "Tu estilo, tu flow." Differentiator vs. a generic clothing store: unisex fit across the whole catalog, editorial/premium presentation instead of a commodity e-commerce look, and national shipping from a Tucumán-based operation.

## Operating Context

Social-commerce model: Instagram and WhatsApp are the actual sales channels; the landing page is the premium storefront that drives traffic there. No cart, no payment flow on-site.

## Product Principles

- Editorial and premium over generic e-commerce: this is explicitly the brief's top concern.
- Motion earns its place: the hero's carousel/depth/transition language should feel intentional and premium, not decorative excess.
- Unisex is structural, not a footnote: layout and imagery should present the catalog as genuinely for everyone, not menswear-with-a-unisex-label.
- Every path leads to WhatsApp or Instagram: since there's no checkout, CTAs must consistently funnel there.
- Real assets only: never substitute stock imagery or invented product shots for the provided library.
