## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Drifting's site reads as a hand-set street-culture magazine cover, not a product grid — scattered, angled cover-lines carry product and offer instead of the neat centered hero-then-grid every streetwear template ships.

**OWN-WORLD:** Ground near-black (#0E0D0C); fluorescent pink (#FF3D8A) and acid green (#C6FF3D) as the two "hot inks," used at 30-60% coverage on key fields, never both diluted to accents; bone/off-white (#F3F1EC) for body copy plates. Hand-inked display face for the masthead/headlines (Bebas Neue or similar condensed slab-ink face), a plain workhorse grotesk (Archivo) for body/UI, a tracked mono (Space Mono) for kickers/labels. Cover-lines are short product/offer statements set at 3-4 different rotation angles per section, never on a shared baseline; masthead = logo lockup, top-left, always level. Cheap-print misregistration (1-2px chromatic offset) on hover/focus states.

**STORY:** Visitor lands on a "cover" (hero carousel of the 4 Drifting hero-character renders using the pinned TOONHUB-style carousel mechanic, re-skinned in this palette/type); scattered cover-lines tease collection drops and the "tu estilo, tu flow" claim; scrolling moves through "spreads" (trust bar, colección, propuesta de valor, social proof, guía de talles, FAQ, contacto) built as magazine pages, each ending in a WhatsApp/Instagram action; visitor believes Drifting is a real, distinct, quality streetwear operation and messages the brand.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-viewport hero. Masthead "DRIFTING" top-left, level, small tracked mono tagline beneath. Character carousel occupies the lower 60-90% of the frame per the pinned TOONHUB roles (center/left/right/back with scale+blur+opacity crossfade, 650ms cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)), re-skinned onto the fluorescent-on-black ground. 2-3 scattered cover-line callouts (claim, incentivo, CTA) at distinct angles around the carousel, one flipped to acid green as the primary CTA. Prev/next arrow controls bottom-left in the carousel's own vocabulary.

**FORM:** Own grounded candidate list, assigned index 3 (skate/griptape) was displaced by dealt challenger `posters-covers-sleeves-movida-scene-magazine`, which won both axes (audience identification, product clarity) and became the build. Raise from declined challenger `signals-instruments-split-flap-concourse`: cascading per-character reveal on headline/CTA entrances (letters settle into place rather than fading as a block).
